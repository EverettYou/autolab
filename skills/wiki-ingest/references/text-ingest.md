# Text-source ingest

Use for papers, reproduction records, lab reports, dated records, and other primarily textual sources. They
keep their repository locations, such as `references/<id>/paper.pdf`, `references/<id>/reproduce/`,
`labs/*/REPORT.md`, `wiki/records/<folder>/`, or `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`.

Read the complete source or the complete scientifically relevant portion. Extract:

- concepts, definitions, assumptions, and scope;
- claims and their evidence, uncertainty, and locators;
- methods described at the scientific level;
- limitations, contradictions, follow-ups, and pitfalls;
- relationships to existing wiki pages, implementations, labs, and thesis claims.

Distinguish published claims from reproduction results, lab observations, and new wiki inference. For a
reproduction record, say what was reproduced, what is partial, and what could not be tested here. For
project self-knowledge, summarize ownership and relationships and link to the resource that owns the
operational detail. Cite PDFs by page, section, or figure and text sources by heading or line when stable.
Update implementation pages when a paper clarifies what code is intended to compute, but do not infer that a
repository faithfully implements the paper without code evidence.
