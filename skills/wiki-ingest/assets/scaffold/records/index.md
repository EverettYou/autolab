# Records

Dated history of the project's work, kept as written. A record states what was known on its date; later records and compiled pages may supersede it, but the record itself is not edited. Compiled pages cite records as evidence. The root [wiki index](../index.md) links here as a hub; describe record kinds in this file.

## Folders

Create one folder per kind of record as the need arises, for example `design/` for design notes and plans or `discoveries/` for scouting and study reports, and describe each here.

## Writing a record

- Name it `records/<folder>/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md` and start with an H1 naming the kind and the date.
- Do not edit an existing record to match later understanding. Write a new record that names the one it corrects, and update the affected compiled pages.
